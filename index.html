PART 1: Possessives (Adjectives vs. Pronouns)

Rule: His, her, their, your come before a noun.
His, hers, theirs, yours, mine replace the noun (no noun after them).
Before a nounWithout a noun
This is his coffee.This coffee is his.
This is her phone.This phone is hers.
This is their house.This house is theirs.
This is your breakfast.This breakfast is yours.
